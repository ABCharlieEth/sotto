Private payments for Zcash

Get paid in shielded ZEC with a single link.

Create an invoice, share the link or QR. Your customer pays from any Zcash wallet. You see it arrive, privately, without giving anyone your keys.

  • Non-custodial. Funds go straight to your own wallet. We only hold a viewing key, which can see incoming payments but can never spend them.
  • Private by default. Shielded payments: amounts and parties stay encrypted on chain. Each invoice gets its own address and memo.
  • Built for shops. Live payment page, confirmation tracking, and a webhook to your store when an order is paid.
